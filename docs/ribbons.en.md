## Customizing the Ribbon Menu in Obsidian

As of Obsidian v1.1.0, the ribbon can be customized via the application settings.

## Navigating to the Ribbon Settings

In the left sidebar, select **Appearance**, then scroll down to the **Interface** section on the right.  
Click **Manage** next to **Ribbon menu configuration**.

## Managing Ribbon Buttons

Here you can enable or disable ribbon buttons, including Obsidian’s built-in **Daily Notes** button and plugin-provided buttons such as those for creating a daily folder structure.
